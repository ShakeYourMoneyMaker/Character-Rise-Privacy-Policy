Character Rise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Virtual Game Knight, LLC ("we", "our", "us") handles information in the Character Rise application ("the app") for Android and other platforms.

The short version

We do not have an account system, we do not host a backend for your game content, and we do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs. All of your character data, settings, and app history stay on your device. When you use AI generation, only the text you enter for that request is sent from your device to the AI provider that you choose and configure, so it can produce your result.

1. Data we do not collect

Character Rise does not collect, sell, or rent personal data. Specifically:

  • No account creation or personal identifiers (name, email, phone, ID).
  • No location, contacts, calendar, health, or biometric data.
  • No advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or analytics/telemetry.
  • No crash reporting or error logs are sent to us or to any third party.
  • No in-app purchases and no payment information.

2. Data we store on your device

The app stores your content locally, in the app's private sandboxed storage on your device (and in your device's local storage):

  • Your characters and their data (a local characters.json file).
  • Your preferences, theme, language, and per-character history.
  • API keys for AI providers you add. Keys are encrypted before storage using AES-256-GCM, under a device secret that never leaves the device: the Android Keystore on Android (protected with your device PIN/biometric prompt) or your operating system credential store on desktop. The app never stores an API key in plain text, and we never see it.

This data exists only on your device and is not transmitted to us. You can delete it at any time by deleting the characters file in the app or by uninstalling the app.

3. Data transmitted to AI providers you choose

The core feature sends the text you submit (your prompt and the character details you selected) from your device directly to the AI provider you have configured - by default Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you set, including a model server on your own network. This is required to generate the result you requested.

Each provider then processes your prompt under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Google Gemini: see Google's privacy policy and the Gemini API terms.
  • Anthropic Claude: see Anthropic's Privacy Policy and Commercial Terms.
  • Custom/local endpoints: see that service's own policies.

We do not receive, store, or process the content you send to these providers. We never inspect your prompts or generated output. Transmission is always over HTTPS (or plain HTTP only to a loopback address such as a local model server on your own machine).

4. Content reporting

The app includes a report button so you can flag AI-generated content you find offensive or harmful. A report includes the character name, the text you sent, the generated reply, and your reason. Reports are held on your device in local storage and are sent only if you choose to submit them, to the developer's reporting address (when configured), and are used solely to investigate and improve content safety. You can delete queued reports at any time from the app's About screen.

5. Children's privacy

The app is a utility for tabletop role-playing games, aimed at players and game masters. It is a 13+ rated utility and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Third-party links

The app may open links (for example, to provider documentation or this policy) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for them.

7. Security

All network traffic uses TLS (HTTPS). Stored API keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a device-held secret as described above, and decrypted key material is wiped from memory immediately after use. Device backups of app data are disabled in the Android app manifest (allowBackup="false"), so your characters and key material are not uploaded to cloud backup.

8. Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our servers. Your in-app data is yours and is controlled locally. If you are in the EU/UK or a state with privacy laws (such as California CCPA/CPRA) this policy reflects the same outcome: the app processes no personal data about you.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this address. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
